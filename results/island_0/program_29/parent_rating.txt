<lemma next_eq_div_gcd

    (m (k + 1) : ℚ) / (n (k + 1) : ℚ) = (2 * (m k : ℚ) + 1) / (2 * (n k : ℚ) + 1))
  (k : ℕ) :
  let d : ℕ := Nat.gcd (2 * m k + 1) (2 * n k + 1)

Failure mode: HARD
Recommendation: Prove “equality of reduced fractions implies numerator/denominator are the gcd-quotients” via these intermediate lemmas:
1) **Cross-multiplication**: from the given rational equality, derive an integer/nat equality  
   \[
   m_{k+1}\,(2n_k+1)=n_{k+1}\,(2m_k+1)
   \]
   in `ℤ` (or `ℕ`), after justifying denominators are nonzero in `ℚ`.
2) **Successor coprime**: use `h_coprime (k+1)` to show `Nat.Coprime (m (k+1)) (n (k+1))`.
