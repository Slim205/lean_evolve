  (m n : ℕ → ℕ)

1) **Oddness of the gcd**: Prove `Nat.gcd a b % 2 = 1` assuming `a % 2 = 1` and `b % 2 = 1`.  
2) **gcd divides the difference of its arguments**: From `d = gcd a b`, derive `d ∣ (a - b)` in ℕ, then transport to ℤ.
